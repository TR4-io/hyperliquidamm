## Model and accounting assumptions

- Decisions use the last available snapshot at the UTC minute after one minute of warmup. The first qualifying opposite-aggressor print must have sufficient size and trade strictly beyond the limit. The arrival-time tie is excluded. Cancel/replace becomes effective after 500 milliseconds, with the old order fillable during that delay. Signals and 30-second markouts use as-of book lookups. Executions remain proxies under sampled L2.
- Quote distances are 1.5, 3 and 6 bps, rounded outward using BTC price rules. Current official metadata returned five size decimals for BTC. Each side/depth parent needs 100 opportunities and 10 fills. A top-five imbalance bin and nested signed one-minute return and volatility bins shrink toward that training-only parent using 100 pseudo-opportunities and 10 pseudo-markouts. Fixed bins and thresholds are provisional development choices. Fill probability multiplies conditional signed markout less one maker fee and the marginal quadratic inventory cost. Current inventory influences that cost and outstanding capacity. The highest positive score wins on each side. [Official price rules](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/tick-and-lot-size)
- The account starts with 10,000 USDC, trades 0.001 BTC clips and caps inventory at ±0.01 BTC. The replay values an immediate exit by walking visible opposite-side depth and charging the taker fee. At a 10% drawdown from peak executable equity it cancels quotes and flattens in the simulator. Funding uses the position entering the settlement timestamp. Every day, including cash days, contributes a P&L observation.
- P&L reconciles to position times midpoint movement plus execution relative to contemporaneous midpoint, less maker and taker fees, plus funding. Final liquidation is included. The output includes trade-level markouts, counts, daily net P&L, open inventory at each day end, and maximum drawdown. The stress cases independently double the qualifying size, add 1 bp of required penetration, raise latency to 1,500 ms, or add 1 bp to each fee.
- The current official tier-zero base perp schedule shows 1.5 bps maker and 4.5 bps taker. Historical effective dates and any account-specific discount remain to be verified before final inference. The October 25 pilot is validation/development data. `node pilot.cjs PILOT.json.gz` checks its reported structure and runs the replacement touch replay once, with funding coverage explicitly reported. The user-reported legacy touch result is the regression reference. The replacement uses minute-grid decisions, warmup, reserved cancel capacity, post-only checks, horizon purging and depth-aware exits. Exact legacy replication still needs its original policy/timeline. [Venue fee schedule](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/fees)

## Final evaluation

Training ends August 26, validation ends October 25, and the 65-day final period spans October 26–December 29, 2026 inclusive, all UTC. Keep final outcomes sealed. Freeze the code and model hashes, configuration, complete coverage manifest, transfer of account state at the boundary, benchmark rules and stress cases before scoring. The prespecified primary statistic is mean daily net USDC P&L over cash, with the empirical 5th percentile of 20,000 circular seven-day-block bootstrap means using seed 20261121 as a one-sided lower bound. Three- and fourteen-day blocks are sensitivities. Retain zero-trade days. Promotion requires positive cumulative net P&L and primary lower bound, complete usable coverage and no drawdown halt. Approximately nine weekly blocks limit precision. Forward paper trading follows only if the frozen model passes.
